## 2. Stage 15 Role

**Stage 15 = Market Intelligence & Signal Processing**

Stage 15 is the **Signal + Market Intelligence Processing Layer** for live and near-real-time market signals.

Relationship:
- **Stage 12** = External Research / data acquisition and preparation.
- **Stage 13** = Reasoning + Decision + Recommendation.
- **Stage 14** = Execution + Broker Integration.
- **Stage 15** = Signal + Market Intelligence Processing.

Core separation:

**Signal ≠ Decision ≠ Execution**

Stage 15 does not own decision authority and does not issue orders.

## 5. Ten Approved Boundary Corrections

1. **Item 4 ↔ Item 10:** Signal Strength consumes approved source authority/reliability inputs; it does not create a parallel Authority or Reliability authority.
2. **Item 4:** Historical accuracy contributes to signal strength only through approved inputs; the source reliability model remains Item 10's responsibility.
3. **Item 7:** Live News Awareness consumes live news context; it does not recreate Stage 12 External Research or Source Discovery.
4. **Item 10:** Signal Source Reliability is distinct from Stage 11 Evidence Authority.
5. **Item 12:** Duplicate filtering is signal-level redundancy filtering and does not rebuild Stage 11 evidence duplicate/uniqueness authority.
6. **Item 13:** Signal Enrichment adds available context; it does not perform an independent research authority.
7. **Item 16:** Signal Anomaly Detection concerns market-signal anomalies, not a parallel security authority.
8. **Item 21:** Live Alerts are market/signal alerts; Stage 19 Observability remains responsible for system-health observability alerts.
9. **Item 25:** Manipulation detection is not duplicated as an independent detector; Item 25 consumes relevant detection results and applies signal-safety response/integrity controls.
10. **Item 28 / Item 29:** Runtime performance metrics remain distinct from signal-quality benchmarking, and fallback/recovery does not grant a replacement source new authority.

## 6. Architectural Boundaries with Stages 11–14

### Stage 11 — Evidence Intelligence
Stage 11 owns Evidence + Verification + Calibrated Confidence and its approved intelligence boundaries.

Stage 15 consumes applicable approved outputs and does not rebuild:
- Evidence Authority
- Evidence Verification
- Evidence Quality/Integrity authority
- Calibrated Confidence
- Decision Authority

### Stage 12 — External Research
Stage 12 owns external research acquisition and preparation:
- search
- source discovery
- retrieval
- extraction
- normalization
- research preparation

Stage 15 owns live signal processing and does not recreate Stage 12 Source Discovery or External Research authority.

### Stage 13 — Decision & Reasoning
Stage 13 owns:
- reasoning
- financial analysis
- decision
- recommendation

Stage 15 produces processed/validated signal intelligence for Stage 13.

**Signal Strength ≠ Decision Confidence.**

Stage 15 may calculate signal strength; Stage 13 does not receive that as a decision-authority replacement for upstream confidence/evidence controls.

### Stage 14 — Execution & Broker Integration
Stage 14 owns:
- execution authorization
- risk enforcement at execution
- order construction
- broker integration
- order submission
- execution lifecycle

A Stage 15 signal must not become an order directly.

**Signal → Stage 15 → Stage 13 Decision/Recommendation → Stage 14 Authorization/Execution**

No bypass is permitted.

## 9. Architectural Locks

1. No parallel authority.
2. **Signal ≠ Decision ≠ Execution.**
3. Stage 15 does not issue broker orders.
4. Stage 15 does not rebuild Stage 11 Evidence/Verification/Confidence authority.
5. Stage 15 does not rebuild Stage 12 Source Discovery/External Research authority.
6. Stage 15 does not replace Stage 13 Reasoning/Decision authority.
7. Stage 15 does not replace Stage 14 Execution authority.
8. Fail-Closed applies when signal validity, freshness, integrity, source state, or required context is insufficient.

10. Fallback sources do not gain authority merely by becoming active.
11. Market/signal alerts are distinct from system observability.
12. Signal storage is not Agent Memory.
