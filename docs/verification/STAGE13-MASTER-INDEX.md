## 4. Nine Approved Architectural Corrections

1. **Item 3 — Decision Framework:** changed from rebuilding Decision Intelligence to using Stage 11 Item 14 and is defined as **Decision Reasoning Framework**.
2. **Item 4 — Confidence Calibration:** changed from a new Confidence calculation authority to **Decision Confidence Interpretation** using Stage 11 Item 2.
3. **Item 13 — Decision Confidence Gating:** fixed source-count requirement changed to configurable **Source Sufficiency Requirement**; Stage 11 results are consumed, not recalculated.
4. **Item 15 — Trade Explanation Layer:** uses Stage 11 Item 21 Explainability and does not rebuild it.
5. **Item 25 — Decision Confidence Calibration:** changed to **Outcome-Based Decision Calibration**.
6. **Item 26 — Decision Explainability:** changed to **Decision-Level Explainability**.
7. **Item 28 — Decision Safety & Ethics:** includes the mandatory **Execution Authorization Boundary**.
8. **Item 29 — Decision Audit Trail:** uses existing Audit Infrastructure and creates no parallel Audit Authority.
9. **Item 14 — Trade Recommendation Builder:** **Recommendation ≠ Execution** is mandatory.

## 5. Fixed Architectural Boundaries

1. **Stage 11 = Evidence + Verification + Calibrated Confidence.**
2. **Stage 12 = External Research: search, sources, content, normalization, filtering, and research preparation.**
3. **Stage 13 = Reasoning + Decision + Recommendation.**
4. Stage 13 consumes Stage 11 outputs; it does not rebuild them.
5. Decision Intelligence authority remains **Stage 11 Item 14**; Stage 13 is the Financial Decision Layer above it.
6. No parallel Evidence, Verification, Confidence, Decision, Explainability, or Audit authority may be created.
7. **Fail-Closed** is mandatory.
8. **Recommendation ≠ Execution.**
9. **Execution Authorization Boundary is mandatory.**
10. Stage 13 has no automatic broker/order execution authority.
11. Any learning/refinement is bounded, auditable, versioned, validated, and reversible.
12. Missing or insufficient data/evidence must not be fabricated.

## 6. Final Pipeline

```
User Question
    ↓
Stage 12 — External Research
    ↓
Stage 11 — Evidence + Verification + Confidence
    ↓
Stage 13 — Reasoning + Decision + Recommendation
    ↓
Execution Authorization Boundary
    ↓
Execution Layer (Stages 50+)
```

Stage 12 prepares external research.  
Stage 11 verifies and calibrates evidence.  
Stage 13 reasons over verified inputs and produces decision/recommendation outputs.  
Execution remains outside Stage 13 and requires the separately authorized execution layer.

## 7. Relationship to Stage 11 and Stage 12

### Stage 12 → Stage 11
Stage 12 brings external research material. Stage 11 consumes, verifies, classifies, and calibrates it.

### Stage 11 → Stage 13
Stage 11 supplies verified evidence, verification status, consistency results, and CALIBRATED CONFIDENCE. Stage 13 consumes these outputs for reasoning and decision-making.

### Stage 13
Stage 13 performs financial reasoning, risk analysis, scenario analysis, decision deliberation, recommendation construction, decision explanation, benchmarking, and decision auditing within its approved boundaries.

No stage may silently take another stage's authority.

---

## 8. Constitutional / Safety Locks

- No parallel authority.
- No evidence fabrication.
- No fixed confidence guarantee.
- CALIBRATED CONFIDENCE is consumed as a bounded signal, not presented as certainty.
- Forecasts, probabilities, signals, and recommendations are analytical outputs, not guarantees.
- BUY/SELL/HOLD is a recommendation classification only.
- Recommendation does not authorize an order.
- Execution Authorization Boundary is mandatory.
- No arbitrary self-modification through strategy refinement.
- Fail-Closed on insufficient evidence/data or invalid state.
- This document does not authorize implementation or constitutional closure.
