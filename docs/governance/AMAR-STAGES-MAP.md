**Status:** Living Document — Editable Reference

## A — Philosophy

This document is a living engineering reference. It is **not a frozen constitution** and does not replace the Master Constitution.

- The document is editable: stages, names, boundaries, and mappings may be added, removed, or revised when justified.
- No deletion without a written rationale.
- No addition without a clear engineering reason.
- Every modification must be recorded through the repository history and the modification policy below.
- The Master Constitution remains the Single Source of Truth. This map organizes and records the current stage consolidation; it does not silently amend the Constitution.
- Existing Stages 1–16 are not modified by this map. Their current approved/locked status remains governed by their existing authoritative records.

### Stage 2 Layering Note

Stage 2 is represented as two integrated layers:

1. **Layer 1 — Stage 2 Engine:** `AmarStageTwoEngine` (Multi-Role Deliberation) and `AmarStageTwoHardening`, with the existing Stage 2 tests. This layer is closed and preserved.
2. **Layer 2 — Cognitive Intelligence:** 72 Capabilities, 14 Engines, and 3 Gates defined by `STAGE2-COGNITIVE-INTELLIGENCE.md`, with the 397-addition breakdown in `STAGE2-CAPABILITY-BREAKDOWN.md`. This layer is designed and remains editable.

The integration is additive: Layer 2 sits above Layer 1; existing Layer 1 code and tests are not replaced or deleted. This map does not grant Layer 2 execution, authorization, broker, evidence-validation, or external-research authority owned by other stages.

### Status interpretation

“Draft” means design/discussion only. It does not mean implemented, tested, or constitutionally closed.

## D — Modification Rules

1. **Addition:** Proposal → Review → Approval → Documentation.
2. **Deletion:** A written rationale is required before removal.
3. **Modification:** The previous state must remain recoverable through repository history; no silent rewriting of architectural history.
4. Any change affecting an existing Stage must be checked for boundary and authority conflicts with other Stages.
5. No new Stage or addition may create a parallel authority for Evidence, Research, Decision, Authorization/Execution, or other constitutionally owned responsibilities.
6. Counts are not fixed by this map; they must be verified when the underlying design is finalized.
7. This map may be revised when implementation findings or owner decisions justify a change.

## E — Parking Lot

Future ideas that are not yet assigned as approved Stage scope belong here until reviewed:

- New market/data-source integrations not yet assigned to an existing Stage.
- Additional media formats or processing capabilities discovered during implementation.
- New research/search adapters that do not yet justify a dedicated architectural boundary.
- Additional bot-learning or evolution methods pending evidence, safety, and compatibility review.
- New UI capabilities pending confirmation of their runtime/generation boundary.
- Infrastructure optimizations whose ownership has not yet been established.

Parking Lot items are **ideas only**. They are not approved scope and must not be implemented as architectural facts without the modification process.
